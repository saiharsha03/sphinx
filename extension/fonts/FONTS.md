# Bundled fonts (extension)

These are the same files as the app's (see `app/src/main/fonts/FONTS.md`), kept under their original names. They come
from Google Fonts' GitHub, commit `e7db6ca603fd4862010383ff17ffd6cc4ffac1c0`, and are under the SIL Open Font License
1.1 (`OFL-*.txt`). They are TTF because Google Fonts' repository ships no woff2; Chrome loads TTF in `@font-face`.
